# The good girl reading list

An adult discovers how much curiosity they edited out of themselves.

Tone: Taboo, defiant. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults raised to be agreeable and avoid awkward subjects.

## Alternate opening hooks

- My "good girl" era had a suspiciously short reading list.
- I was allowed to know everything except what I actually wanted to know.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> My "good girl" era had a suspiciously short reading list.

Visual: Adult narrator's notebook with several crossed-out topics.

### Slide 2

> Politics was rude. Desire was inappropriate. Anger meant I needed an attitude adjustment.

Visual: Three old-looking sticky notes on a closed book.

### Slide 3

> I became very good at having no follow-up questions.

Visual: A dinner place setting with an empty speech bubble.

### Slide 4

> Then someone asked what I wanted to learn. I genuinely did not know what I was allowed to say.

Visual: Blank course-topic field, no logo yet.

### Slide 5

> I wrote "the history of banned books." It felt like starting with the evidence.

Visual: Notebook opens to that exact phrase.

### Slide 6

> The list got longer. Who banned them? Who kept reading? What survived?

Visual: Questions added in different ink colours.

### Slide 7

> I turned it into a Learnfold course. Asked for competing accounts, not a comfortable answer.

Visual: Actual course plan, focused on literary history and censorship.

### Slide 8

> My reading list is becoming difficult. I like it here.

Visual: The original notebook, now full. No victory pose required.

## Why the swipe earns its place

The taboo has substance: who gets to ask questions and choose what to read. The app hosts an inquiry rather than providing a ready-made ideology.

## Caption

Fictional POV from an adult unlearning the need to ask permission. What were you discouraged from being curious about?

## Course prompt for the app shot

> Create a course on the history of banned books and censorship across different societies. Compare specific cases and sources, distinguish fact from interpretation, and avoid treating every case as identical.

This is a proposed example course. Create it and capture the actual result before producing the app slide.

## Production note

Adult framing throughout. If depicting real books or bans, verify each historical claim during production.
