# The most embarrassing folder

A fake scandal reveals a collection of basic questions.

Tone: Confessional, warm, slightly filthy setup. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults who hide gaps in their knowledge.

## Alternate opening hooks

- If you find the hidden folder on my phone, please have some compassion.
- My phone has a folder I would rather explain literally anything else than.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> If you find the hidden folder on my phone, please have some compassion.

Visual: Phone in a dark room. An invented folder icon, no real private files.

### Slide 2

> It is not nudes. It is questions I was too embarrassed to ask.

Visual: Folder opens to handwritten question cards.

### Slide 3

> How does electricity know where to go? Why does the moon follow the car?

Visual: Two question cards. Let them be genuinely endearing.

### Slide 4

> I am 29. I have nodded through explanations of both.

Visual: Birthday candle beside a blank notebook.

### Slide 5

> Every time I tried to catch up, the explanation contained five more words I did not understand.

Visual: A paragraph turning into a maze of underlined words.

### Slide 6

> So I started with one question and admitted exactly where I got lost.

Visual: One card pulled out of the pile: "start here".

### Slide 7

> It became my first Learnfold course. I can ask the follow-up without performing an apology first.

Visual: Real beginner electricity course and an actual follow-up question in the course chat.

### Slide 8

> The folder is still embarrassing. It is also getting answers.

Visual: Folder renamed "working on it". Quiet ending.

## Why the swipe earns its place

The sexual misdirection resolves early into a relatable secret. Learnfold is useful because questions can become a course with context.

## Caption

Fictional adult POV. Everyone has a question they think they should already know the answer to. Mine involved the moon.

## Course prompt for the app shot

> Create an introductory course on electricity for an adult beginner. Start with what a circuit is. Explain new terms before using them, and help me work through follow-up questions.
