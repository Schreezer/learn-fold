# The Wikipedia tab in my will

Mortality interrupts an indefinitely postponed interest.

Tone: Existential, dry. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults with a subject they have postponed for years.

## Alternate opening hooks

- If I die tonight, someone please close the tab called "how do magnets work."
- I have been "meaning to learn that" for eleven years.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> If I die tonight, someone please close the tab called "how do magnets work."

Visual: A fictional browser tab. Black background, deadpan typography.

### Slide 2

> It has been open since February. I keep moving it to the end.

Visual: Calendar pages and one stubborn tab.

### Slide 3

> I know which celebrities hate each other. The magnets remain a mystery.

Visual: Abstract gossip notifications crowding the tab. No real allegations.

### Slide 4

> At some point, "one day" became my entire education plan.

Visual: The words "one day" repeated on a calendar.

### Slide 5

> So I gave the magnets an evening.

Visual: Desk lamp on, ordinary fridge magnets on the desk.

### Slide 6

> My first question was embarrassingly basic. The second was much better.

Visual: Notebook with "why only some metals?" written down.

### Slide 7

> Now there is a magnetism course in my Learnfold library. The tab can finally retire.

Visual: Real course-library screenshot with the example course.

### Slide 8

> If I die tonight, tell everyone I made progress on the magnets.

Visual: One magnet holding up a handwritten "in progress" note.

## Why the swipe earns its place

A mildly morbid hook lands on a tiny, believable action. The repeated magnet joke closes the story.

## Caption

Fictional POV. The tab had a longer lease than my last apartment. What question has been living rent-free in your browser?

## Course prompt for the app shot

> Build a beginner course explaining magnetism from everyday observations. Assume I remember very little school physics. Start with fridge magnets and why they attract some materials.

This is a proposed example course. Create it and capture the actual result before producing the app slide.

## Production note

Keep mortality abstract and comic. No self-harm imagery, imminent-threat framing, or claims that an app resolves existential distress.
