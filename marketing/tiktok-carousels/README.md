# Learnfold TikTok carousel stories

Twelve fictional stories about the messy reasons people get curious. Each has eight slides, alternate opening hooks, visual direction, caption copy, and a proposed course prompt for the app shot. These are scripts, not produced images or real customer testimonials.

The creative premise is that curiosity does not always arrive looking respectable. Sometimes it arrives as jealousy, a crush, wounded pride, or a question you were told not to ask. Let the narrator be a little embarrassing. Give them something specific to discover.

## Start with these

1. [The hate-follow syllabus](01-the-hate-follow-syllabus.md). "I hate-followed her so hard I accidentally got an education." Petty setup, architectural awakening, clean ending. My first pick for a funny brand introduction.
2. [The foreplay was a follow-up question](02-the-foreplay-was-a-follow-up-question.md). "The foreplay was a follow-up question." Attraction leads to an astronomy course and a telescope callback. The most overtly flirtatious concept.
3. [The dark romance footnote](10-the-dark-romance-footnote.md). "Came for the morally grey man. Stayed because the castle made no sense." A reader interrupts her own fantasy to investigate the household finances. Strong fit for adult book audiences.

These are editorial picks, not predictions of reach. Start with one, then compare a second opening hook while holding the rest of the script and presentation steady.

## How to produce them

- Use a vertical 1080 × 1920 canvas as the working design. Keep text away from the outer edges and check the actual TikTok preview for interface overlap.
- Keep each numbered slide as one image. Line breaks may change; the story should not gain a paragraph of explanation.
- Let slide 1 create a precise unanswered question. Slide 2 must advance the situation. Avoid repeating the hook as filler.
- Use a recurring prop to connect the frames: a receipt, gallery ticket, mug, browser tab, or dinner napkin. The last slide brings it back with a different meaning.
- Change the image or composition as the emotional beat changes. Do not make eight identical quote cards. A quiet text-only frame can make a reversal land.
- Show Learnfold once, on slide 7. Use a real course plan, lesson, or course chat. Give the last slide back to the story.
- Keep adult casting and fictional POV framing visible in the finished post. The captions already identify the scenes as fictional. Do not repackage them as real user testimonials or planted organic recommendations.
- For the body-image concept, use object photography and the narrator's experience. Its ending does not claim a cure or make the audience feel defective.
- Use your own or licensed imagery and sound. Each script works without audio. Specific songs and trending audio are intentionally left open for production.

Use a readable, restrained type system. Black, warm off-white, and occasional Learnfold green can hold the series together. Let the romance story feel warm, the gallery story airy, and the group-chat story cramped. Brand consistency does not require identical lighting in every story.

## Soft promotion and product grounding

The product connection is always a specific question becoming a personal course. Current repository material describes topic, link, document, and photo inputs; a proposed course plan for review; progressive lessons; a course library; and questions in the context of a course. These scripts mainly use topic-to-course, the plan, and course questions.

Grounding reviewed on 2026-09-07:

- [Repository overview](../../README.md)
- [iOS product description](../../apps/ios/fastlane/metadata/en-US/description.txt)
- [Course planning tools](../../apps/ios/Sources/Litter/Models/CourseAgentTools.swift)

The course subjects in these scripts are proposed examples, not a claim that those courses ship with Learnfold. Generate the example, inspect the content, then capture the actual app. Source review grounds the concept; it is not runtime verification of a screenshot or a particular generated lesson.

Avoid adding claims about guaranteed expertise, mental-health treatment, grades, romantic success, free access, offline availability, or universal privacy. None is needed for these stories to work. Use a verified current destination if adding a profile link later; no store availability claim is baked into the copy.

## Slide limit

These original stories deliberately stay at eight slides, within the initial requested maximum of ten. Future carousels can be longer when the explanation earns it; see the [flexible length and generation plan](../wait-how.md#slide-lengths-and-generation-budget). TikTok's current official photo-post API documentation permits up to 35 photo URLs, so the original ten-slide limit was a creative constraint, not the platform's general documented photo-post ceiling. Checked 2026-09-07. [TikTok photo-post documentation](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-photo-post)

## First test

Produce 01, 02, and 10 with comparable text size and pacing. Look at completion and shares where analytics expose them, and at profile visits as a sign of product interest. Read the comments: are people discussing the premise, naming something they want to learn, or only reacting to the sexual wording?

Then replace only the opening hook on the strongest story. Treat small samples as directional. If people like the story but miss the product, make the real app crop on slide 7 easier to read before adding a sales pitch to every frame.
