# The family group chat heretic

A forbidden question becomes a private intellectual project.

Tone: Taboo, tense, quietly rebellious. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults raised in families where questioning beliefs was uncomfortable.

## Alternate opening hooks

- The family group chat was fine until I asked for a source.
- Apparently "how do we know?" was the disrespectful part.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> The family group chat was fine until I asked for a source.

Visual: Fictional family chat. No religion, ethnicity, or real relatives identified.

### Slide 2

> An uncle forwarded a claim. Twelve people sent folded hands.

Visual: Abstract message bubbles. Do not reproduce real misinformation.

### Slide 3

> I asked, "Where did this come from?" My mother called immediately.

Visual: Incoming call prop beside the question.

### Slide 4

> Apparently I had embarrassed everyone by being confused in public.

Visual: An empty dining chair. Give the line space.

### Slide 5

> I could have sent a smug rebuttal. I did not actually understand the subject yet.

Visual: Half-written reply, then a blank notebook.

### Slide 6

> So I started with the history. Including the parts that disagreed with each other.

Visual: Two differently annotated pages, no fabricated quotations.

### Slide 7

> I used Learnfold to make a course from the questions I was afraid to ask at dinner.

Visual: Real course plan with a neutral history-of-ideas topic.

### Slide 8

> Next dinner, I had a better question. I asked it anyway.

Visual: Same dining chair, now occupied. Quiet confidence.

## Why the swipe earns its place

The promise is room to investigate. The story does not need the narrator to defeat or abandon their family.

## Caption

Fictional POV. Some family traditions include not asking where the tradition came from. Which subject did you only get to explore as an adult?

## Course prompt for the app shot

> Help me build a beginner course on the history of a belief or tradition I choose. Distinguish primary sources, scholarly interpretations, and disputed claims. Include contrasting perspectives without assuming my conclusion.

This is a proposed example course. Create it and capture the actual result before producing the app slide.

## Production note

Keep the family fictional and the belief unspecified unless a later version is researched. No invented historical facts or straw-man religious claims.
