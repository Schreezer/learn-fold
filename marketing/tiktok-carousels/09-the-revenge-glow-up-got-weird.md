# The revenge glow-up got weird

The expected appearance makeover turns into an absorbing skill.

Tone: Spiteful, comic. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults tempted to turn a breakup into a public performance.

## Alternate opening hooks

- My revenge glow-up has become deeply inconvenient for the revenge.
- I wanted my ex to regret it. Then I got distracted by synthesizers.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> My revenge glow-up has become deeply inconvenient for the revenge.

Visual: An unsent comeback caption on a phone.

### Slide 2

Visual: Calendar note reading "mysterious photo". No body transformation imagery.

### Slide 3

### Slide 8

## Why the swipe earns its place

A revenge arc becomes fun enough to forget its intended audience. It ends with practice, not a promised transformation.

## Caption

Fictional POV. Spite started the project. The bass sound is keeping it alive. What hobby derailed your dramatic comeback?
