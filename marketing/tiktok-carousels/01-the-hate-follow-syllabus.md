# The hate-follow syllabus

Jealousy becomes an interest that survives the person who sparked it.

Tone: Sharp, petty, funny. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults who have hate-followed an ex's new partner.

## Alternate opening hooks

- I hate-followed her so hard I accidentally got an education.
- My ex upgraded. Apparently I needed a syllabus.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> I hate-followed her so hard I accidentally got an education.

Visual: A thumb hovering over a fictional profile. No real account or identifying details.

### Slide 2

> My ex's new girlfriend had opinions about buildings. I had opinions about her.

Visual: An architectural photo beside a half-written, unsent text.

### Slide 3

> She posted a concrete library. Called it beautiful. I needed to know what her problem was.

Visual: A dramatic concrete facade. Tiny handwritten question mark.

### Slide 4

> So I started learning about brutalism. Purely to be a better hater.

Visual: Search phrase on a paper scrap. Transition from profile imagery to architecture.

### Slide 5

> Unfortunately, the library was beautiful.

Visual: Full-bleed building photo. Let the image carry the reversal.

### Slide 6

> Then I wanted to know why everyone hated buildings I was starting to love.

Visual: Two contrasting building photos with no lecture text.

### Slide 7

> Now I have a Learnfold course on architecture. I forgot to check her profile.

Visual: Real course-plan screenshot after creating the example course. Keep the app crop small.

### Slide 8

> She can keep him. I want to see the library.

Visual: Train window, building in the distance. No download button.

## Why the swipe earns its place

The story cashes out the jealousy with a specific discovery. Learnfold holds the new interest; it does not promise romantic revenge.

## Caption

Fictional POV. An embarrassing origin story for a perfectly good interest. What did you start learning for the wrong reason?

## Course prompt for the app shot

> Build me a beginner course on brutalist architecture. Start with why exposed concrete divides people. Use examples of public libraries, and help me form my own opinion.

This is a proposed example course. Create it and capture the actual result before producing the app slide.

## Production note

Play the narrator's pettiness for laughs. The other woman has done nothing wrong.
