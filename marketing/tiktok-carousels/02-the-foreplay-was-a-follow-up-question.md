# The foreplay was a follow-up question

Adult attraction through patient curiosity, with a dating twist.

Tone: Suggestive, warm. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults who find genuine interest attractive.

## Alternate opening hooks

- The foreplay was a follow-up question.
- He said "want the long explanation?" I moved dinner.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> The foreplay was a follow-up question.

Visual: Two wine glasses, fully clothed adult hands. Warm light.

### Slide 2

> We were on a date. He mentioned a star that might already be dead.

Visual: Restaurant window and one distant point of light.

### Slide 3

> I said, "Wait. How would we still see it?"

Visual: One line of dialogue on a napkin.

### Slide 4

> He put his phone away. Drew something on the receipt.

Visual: Hand-drawn light path. Keep it atmospheric, not a scientific diagram.

### Slide 5

> I asked three more questions. He looked happier each time.

Visual: Receipt with more scribbles. Faces can remain out of frame.

### Slide 6

> The next morning I still wanted to understand the star thing.

Visual: Daylight, cold coffee, the receipt on a kitchen counter.

### Slide 7

> So I made a Learnfold course about the night sky. I have questions for date two.

Visual: Actual course plan framed by the saved receipt.

### Slide 8

> "Come over?"
> "Yes. Bring your telescope."

Visual: Fictional text exchange. Small "fictional POV" label.

## Why the swipe earns its place

The app enters when curiosity continues after the date. The final callback belongs to the romance.

## Caption

Fictional POV. Patience is doing a lot for me lately. What subject could someone talk about for an hour and keep your attention?

## Course prompt for the app shot

> Create a beginner astronomy course starting with why looking into space means looking into the past. Assume no physics background. Let me ask follow-up questions as I learn.

This is a proposed example course. Create it and capture the actual result before producing the app slide.

## Production note

Cast adults. Implication and dialogue do the work; no explicit imagery or claims that learning guarantees attraction.
