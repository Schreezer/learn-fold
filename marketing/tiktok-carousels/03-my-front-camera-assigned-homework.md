# My front camera assigned homework

Appearance insecurity turns into curiosity about how images are made.

Tone: Vulnerable, unsettling. Eight slides. Fictional adult POV, written for a brand-owned account.

Audience: Adults exhausted by inspecting their own photos.

## Alternate opening hooks

- I spent 40 minutes investigating a face I have lived in for 27 years.
- My front camera had a complaint. Naturally, I opened a case.

Use the first hook in the script below. Test the second by replacing slide 1 only.

## Slide script

Only the quoted copy goes on the slide. Visual notes are production direction.

### Slide 1

> I spent 40 minutes investigating a face I have lived in for 27 years.

Visual: Bathroom counter, phone face down. Do not show a body-checking collage.

### Slide 2

> I took another selfie. Then another. None of them agreed with each other.

Visual: Three empty photo frames, not magnified facial features.

### Slide 3

> I was about to buy something to fix a problem I could not even describe.

Visual: Closed cosmetic shopping tab rendered as a fictional prop. No product names.

### Slide 4

> Then I thought about passport photos. Nobody looks like themselves in those either.

Visual: Blank passport-photo booth seat. A dry visual joke.

### Slide 5

> I wanted to understand what a camera was actually doing.

Visual: Camera beside an ordinary mug under a desk lamp.

### Slide 6

> Distance. Lighting. Perspective. Suddenly I had questions beyond "what is wrong with me?"

Visual: Photograph the mug from different distances. Keep demonstrations off bodies.

### Slide 7

> I put those questions into a Learnfold photography course. Started experimenting with a mug.

Visual: Real beginner course screenshot next to the mug.

### Slide 8

> I still have difficult photo days. At least the mug has stopped being photogenic about it.

Visual: A comically bad mug photo. Finish gently, without a makeover.

## Why the swipe earns its place

The turn redirects scrutiny toward a learnable craft. The ending admits the feeling is not magically resolved.

## Caption

Fictional POV about a bad photo day. The mug has requested creative control. What ordinary thing would you photograph first?

## Course prompt for the app shot

> Build a beginner course about photographic perspective, camera distance, focal length, and lighting. Use objects for demonstrations and practical exercises. Avoid beauty ratings or appearance advice.

This is a proposed example course. Create it and capture the actual result before producing the app slide.

## Production note

This touches body-image distress without diagnosing body dysmorphia or selling an app as treatment. No flaw circles, body comparisons, beauty scores, weight-loss arc, or cure claim.
