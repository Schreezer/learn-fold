# Saved for Someday

Optional campaign for adults who collect interests, save tutorials and keep putting off the first attempt. The main account direction is now [Wait, How? by Learnfold](wait-how.md), which welcomes curiosity without requiring procrastination or dissatisfaction. Retain the ideas below as campaign material; a separate account is not required.

## Positioning

Audience feeling: "I save everything that interests me. I never actually learn any of it."

Account promise: stories about finally trying the things you keep saving.

Learnfold connection: choose one question or interest and turn it into a personal course. Show a modest first step rather than promising a total lifestyle transformation.

Curiosity Quench positions itself around replacing scrolling with action through interest discovery and action plans. That suggests an adjacent audience for Learnfold. This is an inference from its positioning, not evidence that its customers will convert to Learnfold. [Curiosity Quench](https://www.curiosityquench.com/), reviewed 2026-09-07.

## Creators to follow and study

- Adults trying hobbies such as painting, astronomy, photography and music.
- Digital minimalism creators telling personal stories about wanting their evenings back.
- Beginners showing awkward first attempts and small discoveries.
- People rediscovering childhood interests.
- Curious explainers who make ordinary objects or experiences interesting.

Search phrases: "saved videos never watched", "finding hobbies as an adult", "life after work", "learning for fun", "trying something new", and "rediscovering childhood hobbies".

Follow selectively. Save examples whose audience would plausibly enjoy this account. Read comments for specific frustrations and interests, and engage when you have something to add. Browsing is community research; there is no assumed warm-up duration or engagement quota that guarantees distribution. ## Tone and visual direction

Slightly uncomfortable recognition, followed by a small hopeful action. Let the narrator admit procrastination without treating the viewer as defective.

Suggested style for future generations: contemporary slice-of-life manhwa, soft ink, warm paper texture, muted blue-grey evenings shifting toward apricot daylight. Everyday rooms, cluttered notebooks and unfinished projects. Keep this visually separate from the gothic book account and candlelit dating account.

Show imperfect effort. A first drawing, an unanswered follow-up question or a beginner's experiment can be enough of an ending. Do not imply Learnfold treats addiction or attention disorders.

## Hook bank

- "My saved folder has a more interesting life than I do."
- "I watched someone learn piano for six months. I still cannot play a note."
- "Someone asked what I do for fun. I named apps."
- "I miss being interested in things without needing to be good at them."
- "I have 400 videos saved for the person I keep planning to become."

## First carousel draft

Eight slides. Fictional adult POV. Images have not been generated for this story.

### Slide 1

> My saved folder has a more interesting life than I do.

Visual: An adult on the sofa looking at a phone. Little illustrated glimpses of painting, music and astronomy surround the screen.

### Slide 2

> Apparently I am going to learn piano, paint watercolours, and understand the night sky.

Visual: Three saved-interest cards. Make the imagined activities attractive but ordinary.

### Slide 3

> Tonight I watched someone organise their paintbrushes. I do not own paintbrushes.

Visual: Close-up of the narrator watching a neatly arranged art desk while their own table is empty.

### Slide 4

> I went to save another tutorial. There were already 83 in the folder.

Visual: A fictional folder counter and the narrator's arrested thumb. This is a story prop, not a claim about a real person.

### Slide 5

> So I picked drawing. Found a pencil. Put a mug on the table.

Visual: The same room, now with the phone put down and three ordinary objects on the table.

### Slide 6

> The handle looked wrong. For once, I had a question from actually trying.

Visual: An uneven pencil sketch and a real mug. The narrator leans forward, interested.

### Slide 7

> I started a beginner drawing course in Learnfold. First question: why does my mug look flat?

Visual: An actual example course or clearly illustrated conceptual course card. Keep the rough drawing visible. No fabricated detailed app interface.

### Slide 8

> The drawing was terrible. It was also mine.

Visual: The small drawing pinned above the desk. Warm light. No makeover or grand achievement.

## Caption and example course prompt

Caption: "Fictional POV. My saved folder has excellent taste and no work ethic. What is one thing you keep meaning to try?"

Course prompt: "Build a beginner drawing course using a pencil and ordinary household objects. Start with observing shapes and drawing a mug. Explain new terms simply and suggest small practice exercises."
