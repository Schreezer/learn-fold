Learnfold is a native iOS learning workspace that turns a topic, link, document, or photo into a personal course you can explore, question, and build on.

Start from what caught your attention. Learnfold helps establish your learning goal and starting point, creates a concise course plan for your review, and generates lessons progressively instead of producing an entire synthetic textbook at once.

With Learnfold you can:
- Begin with a topic, webpage, document, or photo
- Answer a short diagnostic and review the proposed course plan
- Read and organize course material in a native workspace
- Generate the next lesson with a connected Hermes learning agent or an available Apple on-device model
- Ask questions in the context of the current course
- Choose whether long code lines wrap or scroll horizontally
- Keep working from the course library and return to material you already generated

Learnfold is being developed around a simple principle: learning matters more than content generation. The long-term goal is a course that responds to evidence, explains meaningful changes, and keeps the learner in control.

AI-generated material can be incomplete or incorrect. Review important claims and source material, especially for high-stakes subjects.
