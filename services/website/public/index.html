THE HONEST QUESTIONS

Before you trust it
with your brain.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT? +

A chatbot can answer a question. Learnfold’s job is to maintain a persistent course: diagnose your starting point, teach in sequence, connect questions to misconceptions, revise the right material, and verify whether the change helped.

Does it generate a whole course at once? +

No. Learnfold creates a concise path and the first useful lesson. Later chapters remain provisional so the course can respond to what you actually understand.

Can generated teaching material be wrong? +

Yes. Learnfold is designed around visible sources, citations, uncertainty, and error reporting, but AI-generated instruction still requires judgment—especially for high-stakes subjects.

When can I try it? +

Learnfold is preparing a free alpha. Join the list for occasional progress notes and an invitation when the core learning loop is ready to test.
